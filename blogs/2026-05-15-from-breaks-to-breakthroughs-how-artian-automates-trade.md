Operational "breaks"—data divergences between systems of record—cost financial institutions billions in compliance fines and hundreds of thousands of dollars per hour in downtime. While traditional RPA and raw LLMs fail to address these complex, judgment-heavy workflows, Artian provides a solution with its multi-agent system. This system combines the adaptability of generative AI with the rigor of graph-based workflows, resolving trade breaks in seconds rather than hours.
